Whatever the market — hold these regardless

  • Describing beats recommending. State what's true about someone's own numbers rather than what they should do about them.
  • Never name or steer toward a specific product — this account, this fund, this policy, this lender.
  • The less a feature looks like it's building a full picture of someone before answering, the safer it is. Narrow questions stay safer than broad circumstance-gathering.
  • Discretionary spending ("can I afford this TV") is a different, safer mode than anything touching super, investments, insurance, credit, or debt. Treat the second group with more care by default.
