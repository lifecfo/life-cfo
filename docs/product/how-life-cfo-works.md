# How Life CFO Works

Last updated: 2026-07-12

## In plain English
Life CFO helps households understand their financial situation and think through decisions using the information they choose to connect or enter.

It does not take control away from the user.

Its role is to:
- hold relevant information
- help explain what is stable or changing
- answer financial questions clearly
- help users think through trade-offs

## What Life CFO can see
Life CFO can only use the data available inside the app, such as:
- connected account information
- balances
- transactions
- financial connections
- saved goals
- saved decisions
- notes or questions entered by the user

## What Life CFO cannot see
Life CFO cannot see:
- bank passwords
- bank login credentials
- emails
- messages
- activity outside the app
- information the user has not provided or connected

## When Life CFO thinks
Life CFO does not think continuously.

It reasons only when needed, such as:
- when the user asks a financial question
- when the user enters a decision workflow
- when the product needs to interpret connected financial data for a user-facing feature

Silence is intentional.

## What Life CFO does with data
Life CFO uses data to:
- answer questions
- explain financial position
- compare scenarios
- surface trade-offs
- help users hold onto conclusions and assumptions

It does not:
- sell data
- use data for advertising
- perform hidden profiling
- act autonomously on financial accounts

## What Life CFO is not designed to do
Life CFO is not designed to:
- move money
- make payments
- initiate transfers
- make decisions for the user
- change important stored state without explicit user action

## How answers are produced
Life CFO works by:
1. retrieving relevant household financial data
2. reasoning over that data within the scope of the user's question
3. returning a calm, analytical answer
4. making assumptions visible when needed

The system is designed to support understanding, not to prescribe actions.

## What happens if the user rarely opens the app
That is fine.

Life CFO is designed so that:
- infrequent use is normal
- users are not punished for not checking in
- the product does not rely on constant engagement
- non-use is not failure

## Final principle
Life CFO should make important financial thinking visible without constantly demanding attention.
