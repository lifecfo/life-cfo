## Second finding from tonight, not yet actioned

Two separate, real duplications, both confirmed by direct code
inspection, not assumed:

- **Cash total.** `cashByCurrency()` (in `app/api/money/overview/route.ts`)
  and `cashForCurrency()` (in `lib/money/reasoning/deriveHomeMoneySummary.ts`)
  implement byte-for-byte identical logic — sum `available_balance_cents
  ?? current_balance_cents` across `truth.accounts` — as two separate
  functions, not one shared source. Overview and Money Map each compute
  their own cash total independently, each from its own fresh
  `getHouseholdMoneyTruth()` fetch.
- **Year summary.** `deriveYearMoneySummary()` is called independently
  by both Money Map's route (`app/api/money/map/route.ts`) and Year at a
  glance's route (`app/api/money/year/route.ts`) — same function, but
  two separate calls, each against its own separately-fetched `truth`.
  Money Map surfaces `yearSummary.timing_needed` and
  `yearSummary.larger_scheduled_payments` from its own call; Year at a
  glance shows the full summary from its own.

Neither is a live disagreement today (same logic, same underlying
tables), but both are two separate computations that only happen to
produce matching numbers right now, not one canonical source. Real,
contained fix: extract `cashByCurrency`/`cashForCurrency` into one
shared helper; for the year-summary duplication, consider whether Money
Map should read Year's output rather than deriving its own copy. Not
urgent, but should be logged — these could start disagreeing the moment
data changes between two page loads, without anyone noticing.
